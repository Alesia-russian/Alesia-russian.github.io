About your Russian tutor

I’m Alesia, a Certified Russian tutor. I have taught online for several years, working with students around the world, from first-time learners to advanced speakers who want to sound more natural.

Language comes with history, art and daily life, so our lessons draw on all three alongside grammar and vocabulary.

Every lesson is adapted to your pace, whether you are starting with the Cyrillic alphabet or polishing your fluency for work or literature.

Questions about Russian lessons

Do I need any Russian to start?

No. If you are new to Russian, we begin with the Cyrillic alphabet and pronunciation, at a pace you are comfortable with.

How are the lessons held?

Lessons take place by video call. After each session I share the materials we used.

Can I study if I live outside Russia?

Yes. Lessons are online and open to students worldwide. We agree on a time that works across time zones.

Which levels do you teach?

Beginner, intermediate and advanced, including advanced learners who want to refine their writing and sound more natural.

How do I book a lesson?

Message me on Telegram or send an email with your level and goals. I reply personally to every message.

Do you offer group lessons as well?

Yes!
